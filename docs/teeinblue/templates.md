# ImagenHub Templates

The **Templates** page is your central command center — a bird's-eye view of all your existing AI workflows. Use this page to:

- **Organize** — Review your active templates and their specific model configurations.
- **Edit** — Update prompts, swap AI models, or adjust generation settings for any template.
- **Monitor** — See a summary of past outputs directly within the template's detail view.

![Templates page overview](/teeinblue/image8.png)

## Creating a Template

Start by creating your first template.

1. Create a new template by importing from a preset, or building one from scratch.

   ![Import preset or create new](/teeinblue/image9.png)

2. To create from scratch, click **+ New Template**. You'll be prompted to choose a model first.

   ![Choose model dialog](/teeinblue/image10.png)

3. Set a name for your template, add a prompt, and configure the other settings. The panels are similar to Teeinblue's.

   ![Template details panel](/teeinblue/image11.png)

4. Once saved, all your templates appear in the list.

   ![Template list](/teeinblue/image12.png)

## Testing a Template

Once you've created a template, the next step is to verify it produces the output you want. Click the template you just created to enter the testing interface and begin validating.

- Any images generated during past sessions, as well as your previous test runs, appear in the history panel on the right side of the screen. Click **Try it** to enter the playground and begin testing.

  ![Template detail view with history panel](/teeinblue/image13.png)

- Inside the playground, upload an image and click **Run** to generate your result.

  ![Template playground with Run button](/teeinblue/image14.png)

::: tip Heads up
Make sure your **Aspect Ratio** and **Resolution** are configured correctly before using the template inside Teeinblue — these parameters directly impact output quality and composition.
:::

![Aspect Ratio and Resolution settings](/teeinblue/image15.png)

Once you're happy with the template, you're ready to wire it into Teeinblue.
